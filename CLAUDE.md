# Dissipative quantum many-body scars: ferromagnetic tower under bulk dephasing

## Goal

Study how an exact quantum many-body scar (QMBS) tower decays when the system is coupled to a weak Markovian bath. Main question: **does the scar signal decay at a rate independent of system size L, or one that grows with L?**

Working answer, to be tested numerically:
- **Global quantities** (fidelity, weight in the scar subspace) decay at a rate ∝ γL. This is trivial: the fidelity is extensive and every site is an independent noise channel.
- **Local observables** (revival envelope of the transverse magnetization) should decay at an L-independent rate. Whether interactions make the envelope faster than the free-spin result, and how its lifetime scales as γ → 0, is the non-trivial part.

Background: Mori's weak-dissipation Liouvillian gap (arXiv:2311.10304) predicts that for generic local operators the decay rate stays finite as γ → 0⁺ when L → ∞ is taken first (a quantum Ruelle–Pollicott resonance). The hypothesis here is that scar revivals are *regular* in that limit, with a lifetime that diverges as γ → 0, while the thermal bulk is singular.

## Conventions

- Spin-1/2 chain of L sites. Pauli matrices σ^α_j, α ∈ {x, y, z}; spin operators S^α_j = σ^α_j / 2.
- Basis |↑⟩, |↓⟩ with σᶻ|↑⟩ = +|↑⟩. |±⟩ = (|↑⟩ ± |↓⟩)/√2, so σˣ|±⟩ = ±|±⟩.
- Singlet projector on a bond:
  R_{j,j+1} = 1/4 − **S**_j·**S**_{j+1} = |s⟩⟨s|_{j,j+1}, with |s⟩ = (|↑↓⟩ − |↓↑⟩)/√2.
  Check: R = 1 on the singlet and 0 on the triplet, and R² = R.
- Boundary conditions: periodic (indices mod L, requires L ≥ 4) is the default. Open is also allowed: keep a term only if all the sites it touches lie in 1..L.

## Scar tower

Ferromagnetic multiplet (total spin S = L/2):

  |Φ_n⟩ ∝ (S⁻_tot)ⁿ |↑↑…↑⟩, n = 0, …, L, with S^z_tot |Φ_n⟩ = (L/2 − n)|Φ_n⟩.

These states are symmetric under any permutation of sites, so R_{j,j+1}|Φ_n⟩ = 0 on every bond. On a connected chain they are the *only* states annihilated by all R_{j,j+1}. All tower states have momentum k = 0.

Scar bond algebra (Moudgalya–Motrunich): generated by R_{j,j+1}σ^α_{j+2} and σ^α_{j−1}R_{j,j+1}, α ∈ {0, x, y, z}. Its commutant is span{|Φ_m⟩⟨Φ_n|} ⊕ 𝟙.

## Hamiltonian (baseline)

  H = H_I + h S^z_tot

  H_I = Σ_j [ Σ_{α∈{x,y,z}} ( a_α R_{j,j+1} σ^α_{j+2} + b_α σ^α_{j−1} R_{j,j+1} )
             + Σ_{α,β∈{x,y,z}} c_{αβ} σ^β_{j−1} R_{j,j+1} σ^α_{j+2} ]

- Every term is Hermitian: the factors act on disjoint sites and commute.
- Every term annihilates the tower (Type I, i.e. a sum of local annihilators). H_I is in class zero.
- h S^z_tot is the lifting term. It makes the tower equally spaced:
  **H|Φ_n⟩ = h(L/2 − n)|Φ_n⟩** exactly.
- **Deliberately excluded from the baseline:** the bare J·R_{j,j+1} term (that is just Heisenberg) and the Dzyaloshinskii–Moriya term Σ(**S**_j × **S**_{j+1})·**n̂**. The DM term can be added later as a single knob to compare Type II classes; it needs periodic boundaries.

Current coefficients (`rust/src/parameters.rs`, since 2026-10-14):
- h = 1
- a = (a_x, a_y, a_z) = (1, 1, 1), b = (b_x, b_y, b_z) = (1, 1, 1)
- c_{xx} = c_{yy} = c_{zz} = 1, all other c_{αβ} = 0
  (c_{αβ} multiplies σ^β_{j−1} R_{j,j+1} σ^α_{j+2})
- Used with random c (`Params::random_c`): on every bond each of c_xx, c_yy, c_zz is multiplied by an
  independent factor uniform in [0.5, 1.5].

Previous coefficients (every result in "Results so far" and "Results with random c" below was obtained with these):
h = 0.5, a = (0.73, 0.41, 0.29), b = (0.58, −0.37, 0.66), c_{xz} = 0.31, c_{zy} = −0.44, c_{yx} = 0.27, all other c_{αβ} = 0.

Symmetries and what breaks them:

| Symmetry | Broken by | Current coefficients |
|---|---|---|
| SU(2) | any dressing (a uniform c ∝ δ_{αβ} alone is SU(2) symmetric) | broken by a, b and the random c |
| U(1) about z | a_x, a_y, b_x, b_y ≠ 0 | broken |
| Spin flips, π rotations, time reversal Π(iσʸ)K | the linear dressings a_α, b_α (odd under spin inversion; R and c-terms are even) | broken |
| Reflection | a_α ≠ b_α, or site-dependent c | a = b, so broken only by the random c |
| Complex conjugation K | a_y, b_y ≠ 0, or any c_{αβ} with exactly one y index | broken (a_y, b_y ≠ 0) |
| Translation | only by site-dependent coefficients | broken by the random c |

H is complex, so the bulk should show GUE statistics. For the current coefficients this has not been checked
(sanity check 2 below).

## Open-system dynamics

Lindblad equation with uniform σᶻ dephasing (jump operators √γ σᶻ_j):

  dρ/dt = −i[H, ρ] + γ Σ_j (σᶻ_j ρ σᶻ_j − ρ)

- With this normalization, a single free spin's transverse coherence decays as e^(−2γt).
- The dissipator does **not** respect the scars. That is intentional: it is the generic-bath case.
- σᶻ_j commutes with h S^z_tot, but not with H_I.
- The dynamics is unital, so the infinite-temperature state 𝟙/2^L is stationary. It is **not** the unique steady state: |↑…↑⟩ = |Φ_0⟩ and |↓…↓⟩ = |Φ_L⟩ are eigenstates of H and of every σᶻ_j, so they are dark states. The kernel of 𝓛 is spanned by 𝟙, |Φ_0⟩⟨Φ_0| and |Φ_L⟩⟨Φ_L| (3 zero eigenvalues in ED for every L checked).

## Initial state

  ρ(0) = |ψ₀⟩⟨ψ₀|, |ψ₀⟩ = |−⟩^⊗L (all spins along −x).

This is a spin coherent state with S = L/2, so it lies entirely in the tower: |ψ₀⟩ = Σ_n c_n|Φ_n⟩ with |c_n|² = C(L,n)/2^L.

## Exact references (use these as tests)

1. **No noise (γ = 0), full H.** Only the lifting term acts on the tower, so the state stays a product state:
   |ψ₀(t)⟩ = ⊗_j (e^(−iht/2)|↑⟩ − e^(+iht/2)|↓⟩)/√2
   ⟨σˣ_j(t)⟩ = −cos(ht), ⟨σʸ_j(t)⟩ = −sin(ht), |m_⊥| = 1.
   The revivals are perfect, with period 2π/h.

2. **Free spins (H_I = 0), with noise.** The spins are independent:
   m_x(t) = −cos(ht) e^(−2γt), m_y(t) = −sin(ht) e^(−2γt), |m_⊥(t)| = e^(−2γt), independent of L.
   F(t) = [(1 + e^(−2γt))/2]^L ≈ e^(−γLt) at short times (extensive).

3. **Scar residual.** ‖H|Φ_n⟩ − h(L/2 − n)|Φ_n⟩‖ must be at machine precision for all n.

## Observables

- m_α(t) = (1/L) Σ_j Tr[σ^α_j ρ(t)] for α = x, y.
- **Envelope** |m_⊥(t)| = √(m_x² + m_y²). This is the main quantity.
  With trajectories, compute it from the *trajectory-averaged* m_x and m_y, not as an average of per-trajectory |m_⊥|.
- Fidelity F(t) = ⟨ψ₀(t)|ρ(t)|ψ₀(t)⟩, using the noiseless product state from reference 1.
  With trajectories: F = mean of |⟨ψ₀(t)|ψ_traj(t)⟩|². Also report the rate function −(1/L) ln F, which is intensive.
- Tower weight W(t) = Σ_n ⟨Φ_n|ρ(t)|Φ_n⟩ (global contrast to the envelope).

## Numerical methods

**Quantum trajectories (primary).** Because (σᶻ_j)² = 1, the effective Hamiltonian is H − iγL/2, i.e. H up to a constant. Each trajectory is therefore exact unitary evolution under H, interrupted by σᶻ_j kicks:
- the waiting time between kicks is exponential with total rate γL;
- the kicked site j is uniform in 1..L;
- the jump probabilities do not depend on the state, so this sampling is exact.

A kick rotates one spin by π about z. Only a fraction 1/L of the kicked state stays in the tower; the rest is k ≠ 0 spin waves, which H_I then thermalizes. Small-k components are asymptotic scars and relax slowly.
- Exact state vectors: L ≈ 20–24.
- MPS (TEBD/TDVP) trajectories: larger L. The state is a product state between kicks, and entanglement grows only near defects.

**Full Lindbladian ED (cross-check).** Superoperator dimension 4^L, so L ≲ 12. Use translation: H, the dissipator and ρ(0) are all translation-invariant, so ρ(t) stays in the zero-momentum sector of the adjoint action (TρT† = ρ). This also gives direct access to the Liouvillian spectrum.

## Sanity checks before any dissipative run

1. Scar residual at machine precision (reference 3).
2. Bulk is thermal: in the k = 0 sector with the L + 1 scars removed, the mean gap ratio r ≈ 0.60 (GUE). If r ≈ 0.53 (GOE), there is a hidden antiunitary symmetry; if r ≈ 0.39 (Poisson), a hidden conserved quantity.
3. Eigenstate entanglement: thermal dome, with the scars as outliers (log L entanglement).
4. Reproduce references 1 and 2 (set the coefficients of H_I to zero for reference 2).

## Planned measurements

1. **L-independence of the envelope.** Plot |m_⊥(t)| vs γt for 3–4 values of L at fixed γ, with e^(−2γt) overlaid. Expectation: curves collapse for L ≫ (defect spreading velocity) × t.
2. **Scaling with γ.** For small γ, test whether the envelope collapses in γt (lifetime ∝ γ^(−1)), γt² (ballistic defect spreading, lifetime ∝ γ^(−1/2)), or γt^(3/2) (diffusive spreading, lifetime ∝ γ^(−2/3)). The light-cone argument behind this is a heuristic: it assumes a defect fully destroys local coherence when it arrives.
3. **Global contrast.** Show that F(t) and W(t) decay at a rate ∝ γL, and that −(1/L) ln F is L-independent.

Controls:
- A generic (non-tower) product initial state at the same energy density should show no revivals.
- Scar-respecting dissipation (jump operators from the algebra, e.g. L_j = R_{j,j+1}σˣ_{j+2}) should show no decay of the tower at all: the tower becomes a decoherence-free subspace.

Later knobs:
- Add the DM term d·Σ(**S**_j × **S**_{j+1})·**n̂** (periodic boundaries) to test whether the Type II class changes the envelope or the bulk relaxation.
- Add range-4 products with other dressings, or site-dependent coefficients, to remove translation.

## Working rules

- Do only the computation that is asked for, simplest case first (γ = 0 as a check, then one parameter at a time). No extra observables, spectra or analyses unless requested.
- The standard measurement is ⟨σˣ⟩, ⟨σʸ⟩, ⟨σᶻ⟩ (site averages) against time from |−⟩^⊗L, at a fixed small γ, comparing sizes L.
- Rust computes and writes CSV to `data/`; Python in `plot_scripts/` reads the CSV and writes figures to `data/`. `data/` is not tracked by git; everything else is.
- Plot scripts need numpy, pandas, matplotlib (on the WSL laptop: `~/anaconda3/bin/python`; the system `python3` has no numpy).

## Conventions in the code (do not change silently)

- Basis states are bit strings, bit j = 0 for |↑⟩ and 1 for |↓⟩. Translation shifts site j → j + 1.
- A Pauli string is two masks: bit j of `x` set for σˣ or σʸ, bit j of `z` set for σᶻ or σʸ; P = i^{|x & z|} X^x Z^z.
- `Params.c[α][β]` multiplies σ^β_{j−1} R_{j,j+1} σ^α_{j+2} (same index order as c_{αβ} above). `Params::baseline` and `Params::random_c` take h, a, b, c, the random-c range and the boundary condition from `src/parameters.rs`; changing them there changes every binary.
- Random c (`Params::random_c`, field `c_bond`): on every bond j, each nonzero baseline c[α][β] is multiplied by an independent factor uniform in [0.5, 1.5]; a and b stay uniform. One realization per L, generator `Rng::new(1000 · seed + L)`: first the c factors, then the cone azimuths φ_j. `disorder`, `probes`, `ed` and `states` all use this same realization.
- Cone states: spin j along n_j = (−cos θ, sin θ cos φ_j, sin θ sin φ_j); θ = 0 is the scar state |−⟩^⊗L.
- Without translation (random c or open chain) the exact method uses the full Pauli basis (`Sector::full`, every string its own orbit, coordinate r_P, dimension 4^L); index of a string = x | z << L. Full basis: L ≤ 14, with a matrix-free application of H for L ≥ 11 (`MATRIX_FREE_MIN_L`; faster than the sparse matrix from L = 11 on the laptop, and the only option at L = 13, 14, where the sparse matrix would need 170–750 GB) (translation orbits remain limited to L ≤ 12).
- Operators in `probes` are evolved forward with 𝓛 in the same coordinates (x_P = Tr(X P)/2^L); autocorrelations use Tr[A e^{𝓛†t}(B)] = Tr[e^{𝓛t}(A) B] for Hermitian A. P3 evolves O^x_π and O^y_π separately and combines them into C⁺ for O⁺ = Σ_j (−1)^j σ⁺_j, so |C⁺| has no precession.
- ED (`ed`): γ > 0 diagonalizes the dense real 4^L generator with `LAPACKE_dgeev` (right and left vectors). γ = 0 is degenerate (every |E⟩⟨E| has λ = 0), so it diagonalizes H with `zheevd` and uses the modes |m⟩⟨n|, λ = −i(E_m − E_n). Weights of a right eigenvector R: tower = ‖PRP‖/‖R‖, mixed = √(‖PRQ‖² + ‖QRP‖²)/‖R‖, bulk = ‖QRQ‖/‖R‖ (squares sum to 1); energy_overlap = |Tr(H U)|/(‖H‖‖U‖) for the left eigenvector U. Dense ED is limited to L ≤ 6 here (L = 7 needs ≈ 7 GB, L = 8 ≈ 100 GB).
- Exact method: ρ = 2^(−L) Σ_P r_P P with r_P = Tr[ρ P] real. The generator is a real matrix: the Hamiltonian part is antisymmetric, dephasing is diagonal, −2γ per site carrying σˣ or σʸ. Only translation orbits of Pauli strings are kept (one orthonormal vector per orbit O of size p_O, coordinate √p_O · r_O); dimension ≈ 4^L / L. Time stepping is a Taylor series of exp(𝓛 dt) summed to round-off, in substeps with row-sum norm ‖𝓛 dt_sub‖_∞ ≤ 4 (`SUBSTEP_NORM`). The matrix-vector product is limited by memory bandwidth (no speedup beyond ≈ 4 threads on the laptop). The orbit code is limited to L ≤ 12 by the orbit table (full basis: L ≤ 14, see above).
- Trajectory H (`trajectory.rs`): Pauli terms grouped by flip mask x (101 masks for 600 strings at L = 20), so ψ(t ^ x)
  is read once per group; H ψ is split over threads from 2^15 amplitudes on. e^{−iH τ}: Taylor substeps with
  1.5 · ‖H‖_est · τ ≤ 4. `states_traj` validated against the exact ρ at L = 8, γ = 0.1 (scar and x-Néel, 2000
  trajectories): rms deviation ≈ 1 standard error.
- Trajectories: σᶻ_j is Hermitian, so the dephasing is unravelled as a real-noise diffusion that keeps the state normalized (a fluctuating z field on every spin). One step is e^{−iH dt/2} · Π_j e^{−i√γ ΔW_j σᶻ_j} · e^{−iH dt/2}, ΔW_j ~ N(0, dt); the error of the average is O(dt²). This is the default (`--unravel diffusive`).
- Jump unravelling (`--unravel jump`, `JumpTrajectory`): the one under "Numerical methods". Waiting times exponential
  with total rate γL, kicked site uniform, σᶻ_k applied exactly; between kicks e^{−iHτ} with the Taylor substeps,
  split only at the kicks and the output grid (dt = min(--dt, 0.05/γ) as for diffusive). From the scar state the
  state before the first kick is the reference-1 product state, written down at the kick time (no state vector is
  allocated before it). Exact sampling: the only error is round-off. Noise seed: `--traj-seed` if given, else
  `--seed` (so the diffusive runs without `--traj-seed` reproduce the earlier ones); the realization always comes
  from `--seed` (`states_realization_L<L>.csv` identical for any traj-seed).
  Validated (2026-10-19) against exact `states`, scar, L = 8, 2000 trajectories: rms deviation of m_x, m_y, m_z
  0.8 – 1.0 standard errors at γ = 0.1 (t ≤ 35.5) and 0.9 – 1.0 at γ = 0.01 (t ≤ 178); max 2.9 standard errors.
  Tests: analytic start = evolution from t = 0 (1e-11), H_I = 0 gives e^(−2γt). Laptop cost (L = 16, γ = 0.1, all
  trajectories kicked): jump / diffusive = 0.79 at output step 0.1, 0.59 at 0.25, 0.63 at 0.5 (the Taylor series over
  a longer step needs fewer H ψ per unit of t, up to the substep limit).
- TDVP: site type `PauliVec` with states (1, σˣ, σʸ, σᶻ). Left multiplication by σ^a is A^a + iB^a on the coefficients, so −i[P, ·] = 2 Im ⊗_j (A^{a_j} + iB^{a_j}), a real MPO; the periodic boundary enters as long-range terms. Observables are divided by the trace coefficient.

  | Γ_∞ | 0.12 ± 0.02 | 0.22 ± 0.06 | 0.36 ± 0.02 | 0.47 ± 0.02 | 0.67 ± 0.02 | 1.28 | 2.26 | 4.21 | 10.1 | 20.1 |

  Local exponent d ln Γ_∞/d ln γ ≈ 0.4 – 0.5 for 0.05 ≤ γ ≤ 0.2 (lifetime ~ γ^(−1/2), over only a factor 4 in γ),
  → 1 at large γ (Γ_∞ → 2γ). At γ ≤ 0.02 the extrapolation is unreliable: slope B ≈ −43γ, and linear and quadratic
  intercepts differ by 2 – 3γ.
- **z-Néel |↑↓↑↓…⟩, |s_z|.** γ = 0: thermal decay Γ_e ≈ 5 (L ≥ 10); L = 4 has strong revivals. Γ_∞ ≈ 5.0, flat for
  γ ≤ 0.05 (= closed-system thermalization); 4.89 (γ = 0.1), 4.72 (0.2), 4.28 (0.5), 3.77 (1), 2.91 (2), 1.71 (5),
  0.98 (10): Zeno slowdown ∝ 1/γ at large γ. Scar and z-Néel Γ_∞ cross at γ ≈ 1.5; at γ = 0.01 the scar decays
  ≈ 40× slower.
- **⟨n⟩ for z-Néel** (number of down spins, ⟨n⟩/L = (1 − m_z)/2). γ = 0: rises from 1/2 to ≈ 0.55 (m_z ≈ −0.1,
  converged for L = 8 … 12; the thermal value is fixed by the energy since H contains h Sᶻ). With γ: overshoot, then
  relaxation back to 1/2 at ≈ 1.5 – 2.3γ, L-independent for L ≥ 8. The runs stop on |s_z|, so ⟨n⟩ is not fully
  relaxed at small γ.
- **Caveat: z-Néel is a biased reference.** It is a fixed point of the dissipator (diagonal in the z basis,
  D(ρ₀) = 0), and s_z is invisible to it (D†(σᶻ_j) = 0); the bath acts only after H_I rotates weight into σˣ/σʸ.
  With H_I = 0, s_z does not decay at all, and at large γ it is Zeno-protected. So it is not like-for-like with the
  scar's m_⊥ (e^(−2γt) at H_I = 0). The thermal reference is now the x-Néel |+−+−…⟩ with s_⊥ = √(s_x² + s_y²):
  same e^(−2γt) baseline at H_I = 0, zero tower overlap of the k = π observable (P_tower O_π P_tower = 0). It is not
  a dark state of 𝓛 (only |↑…↑⟩ and |↓…↓⟩ are). x-Néel results: "Results up to L = 20" above.

## Results so far (γ = 0.01, t ≤ 100, dt = 0.1)

All results in this section and the next were obtained with the previous coefficients (see "Hamiltonian (baseline)").

- **γ = 0 check.** Perfect oscillations ⟨σˣ⟩ = −cos(ht), ⟨σʸ⟩ = −sin(ht), ⟨σᶻ⟩ = 0 to 4e-14 for L = 4 … 10.
- **Decay rate against L.** Amplitude √(⟨σˣ⟩² + ⟨σʸ⟩²) fitted with A₀e^(−Γt) while above 0.05:

  | L | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 | 9 | 10 |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Γ / γ | 3.06 | 3.80 | 4.66 | 5.75 | 6.68 | 7.61 | 8.39 |

  Power law Γ ≈ 0.63 γ L^1.13 (rms residual 1.6 %); exponential in L fits worse (4.6 %). The decay is not a single exponential, so Γ is an average over the fit window (exponent 1.00 – 1.14 depending on the window).
- **Bound.** Every Liouvillian eigenvalue has −2γL ≤ Re λ ≤ 0 (antisymmetric plus diagonal generator), so the rate cannot grow exponentially with L.
- **⟨σᶻ⟩** stays below 0.04 in magnitude.
- **Larger γ** (first-pass runs for L ≤ 8; that code was removed, its outputs are in the untracked `data/first_pass/`): the 1/e rate of the amplitude saturates with L, about 3.8γ at γ = 0.1 and 2.9γ at γ = 0.2, while it grows with L for γ ≤ 0.05. For γ → 0 at fixed L, first-order perturbation theory gives tower coherences |Φ_n⟩⟨Φ_{n+1}| decaying at γL(1 − m_n m_{n+1}), m_n = 1 − 2n/L, with weights C(L−1, n)/2^(L−1); this matched the L = 7, γ = 0.01 spectrum. So the growth with L at small γ is the perturbative finite-size regime, and L ≤ 10 does not decide the L → ∞-first limit.
- **Trajectories** agree with the exact evolution within the statistical error for L = 4 … 8 (2000 trajectories; 20000 at L = 4, 5, error ≈ 1e-3). The spread of one trajectory is ≈ 0.97 / L at its largest.
- **TDVP** does not converge below the full bond dimension 4^(L/2): at L = 6 the error is 1e-2 at χ = 32 and 8e-5 at χ = 64; at L = 8 it is 2e-2 at χ = 64 (t ≤ 10). The bond dimension reaches its cap by t ≈ 0.5.
- **Cost** (one run; `python plot_scripts/resource_tables.py` prints the full tables). Exact: 4.8 min and 0.2 GB at L = 10, about 4× per added site in both, 12.7 GB at L = 13. Trajectories: 31 core-minutes each at L = 16, 10.5 core-hours at L = 20, 48 B × 2^L of RAM each. Cluster figures in those tables rest on assumed, not measured, speedups.

## Results with random c (no translation; γ = 0.01, one realization per L)

- **Scar state with random c** decays like the uniform baseline: amplitude fit while above 0.05 gives Γ/γ = 3.06, 3.89, 4.68, 5.81, 6.69 for L = 4 … 8 (baseline 3.06 … 6.68). γ = 0 oscillations stay perfect on every site (4e-14).
- **Cone states** (`disorder`, θ = 0 … π/2): at γ = 0 the oscillations of ⟨σˣ⟩, ⟨σʸ⟩ persist with a reduced amplitude at θ = π/8, π/4 and are nearly gone for θ ≥ 3π/8, L ≥ 5; with γ they die within a few periods.
