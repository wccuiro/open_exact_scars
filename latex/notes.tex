\title{Ferromagnetic scar tower under bulk dephasing:\\ notes on the results so far}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
We study how the ferromagnetic quantum many-body scar tower of a spin-$1/2$ chain decays under uniform single-site noise of rate $\gamma$ ($\sigma^z$ dephasing, and $\sigma^x$, $\sigma^y$ dephasing, depolarizing, decay and thermal noise), comparing the scar state $\ket{-}^{\otimes L}$ with two non-scar product states, the $x$-N\'eel and $z$-N\'eel states. With exact density-matrix evolution up to $L = 14$ and quantum trajectories up to $L = 22$, for $0.01 \le \gamma \le 10$, the $1/e$ rate of the scar's transverse magnetization becomes $L$-independent for $\gamma \gtrsim 0.05$, and its thermodynamic value $\Ginf$ decreases towards zero as $\gamma$ decreases, as $\gamma^{p}$ with $p = 0.54 \pm 0.06$ over $0.01 \le \gamma \le 0.05$ ($0.49 \pm 0.01$ up to $\gamma = 0.2$). The thermal $x$-N\'eel signal instead decays at the closed-system thermalization rate $\approx 4.8$ for every $\gamma \le 0.1$. A single-kick model, in which every $\sigma^z$ kick damages the coherence as one flip evolved with $H$ does, reproduces the measured rate at every $L = 12$--$20$ and $0.01 \le \gamma \le 0.05$ to within $6\%$, without fit parameters, so the lifetime is fixed by how the damage of a single kick grows in time. The damage spreads ballistically ($v \approx 1.6$) until it wraps around the ring at $L = 24$; continuing it linearly predicts $\Ginf \to (c\gamma/2)^{1/2}$ ($c \approx 2$). Under the other channels the scar rate has the same exponent and collapses onto the $\sigma^z$ curve once $\gamma$ is scaled by the damage of one kick. When the signal is lost, the N\'eel density matrices have scrambled (operator size $\approx 0.7\,L$), while the scar density matrix has the size $L/2$ of the initial product state: the N\'eel signals are lost to the closed-system dynamics, as in Mori's weak-dissipation limit, the scar signal only to the accumulation of kicks.
\end{abstract}
