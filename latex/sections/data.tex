\section{Data computed}
\label{sec:data}

What has been computed with the current coefficients (Sec.~\ref{sec:model}; random $c$, seed $1$ unless stated). All data are in \texttt{data/used\_data/} (not tracked by git), the channel study and the newer runs in its subfolder \texttt{lean/}. The decay rates of every run are in \texttt{states\_rates.csv} and \texttt{channels\_rates.csv}, the extrapolations in \texttt{states\_\allowbreak inverse\_\allowbreak L.csv}, \texttt{states\_\allowbreak inverse\_\allowbreak L\_\allowbreak large.csv} and \texttt{channels\_\allowbreak inverse\_\allowbreak L.csv}.

Every run starts from the state of Table~\ref{tab:states} and stops once its signal has decayed (below $10^{-3}$, or at $1.5\,t_e$ for the channel study and $L = 22$). \paragraph{Single kicks} (\texttt{single\_flip}, scar state, $H$ only; phases $\times$ kicked sites, up to $\tau_{\max}$): $L = 12, 14, 16, 18, 20$, $16$ phases $\times$ all sites, $\tau \le 25$; $L = 22$, $16 \times 4$ sites, $\tau \le 8$; $L = 24$, $16 \times 4$, $\tau \le 8$, and $4 \times 4$, $\tau \le 16$ with block entanglement; seeds $2$, $3$, $4$ at $L = 16$ and $20$, $4 \times 4$, $\tau \le 25$.

\paragraph{Spectra and eigenstates.} All eigenstates of $H$ with their half-chain entropy (\texttt{entanglement}), $L = 6$--$12$. Every eigenmatrix of $\Lv$ with $E_{L,R}$ and $S_{L,R}$ (\texttt{lindblad\_entanglement}), $\gamma = 0$ for $L = 4$--$7$ and $\gamma = 0.01$ for $L = 4$--$6$. The Liouvillian spectrum of the open chain (\texttt{ed --open 1}), $L = 4, 5, 6$, $\gamma = 0.01$, used as the reference of CLIK.

\paragraph{Tensor networks} (Appendix~\ref{app:other}). CLIK, $\gamma = 0.01$: dense backend $L = 4, 5, 6$ (ring, random initial states, $T \le 400$) and $L = 6$ (open chain, random and scar initial states, $T \le 200$); MPS backend $L = 6$ open chain, $\chi = 16, 32$ and the full $64$. Forward/backward evolution: $L = 6$, full $\chi$, $t \le 5$, the three states, $\gamma = 0$ and $0.1$, $\sigma^z$, $\sigma^x$ dephasing and decay, against the exact \texttt{states} runs.
